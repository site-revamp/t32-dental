The T32 approach

Dentistry should feel considered, connected and centred on you.

More than a dental appointment.

T32 Dental Group brings general and specialist dental disciplines together to support patients with different needs—from regular check-ups and preventive care to restorative, cosmetic and specialist treatment.

The aim is simple: help you understand your options, make informed decisions and receive care that is appropriate to your individual dental health.

Multi-specialty dentistry

When your needs are complex, expertise matters.

Some dental concerns benefit from specialist assessment. T32's specialist services include prosthodontics, periodontics and orthodontics, alongside other areas of dental care.

Prosthodontics Restoration, replacement and oral rehabilitation
Periodontics Gum and supporting bone health
Orthodontics Teeth alignment and bite concerns
Coordinated care Specialist input where clinically appropriate
